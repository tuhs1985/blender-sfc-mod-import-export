# Parking lot

## Remix-specific material options

Explore allowing artists to add material information for the user's Remix project alongside the OP diffuse and illumination maps. Blender material nodes or properties may be useful, but the desired options and how they should be retained are not yet defined.

It is unknown whether Orion Pirates ignores extra material data or how the MOD exporter should handle it. Do not assume the game accepts extra slots or fields; investigate and test before changing the OP format or export behavior. Keep this idea out of the initial OP importer/exporter scope until it is specified.
